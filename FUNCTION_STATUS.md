## What the stages mean

- **Planned:** Intended behavior, not yet demonstrated.
- **Foundation tested:** A supporting component has passed controlled checks.
- **Prototype testing:** Working pieces exist, but the complete capability remains unproven.
- **Reconciliation pending:** Evidence and changes need final integration review.
- **Long-term objective:** A direction to investigate after earlier milestones are proven.

## Current focus

The priority is to finish approved engineering work, verify integration readiness, and establish a genuine end-to-end Night Shift result. Individual passing tests are useful evidence, but they are not equivalent to a finished autonomous system.
